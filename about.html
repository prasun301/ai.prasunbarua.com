About Prasun AI

What is Prasun AI?

Prasun AI is a web-based AI assistant created to make everyday use of artificial intelligence simple and accessible. It provides a straightforward place to ask questions, explore ideas, learn new concepts, work through problems, and get help with a wide range of tasks.

Whether you need an explanation, are working on a writing project, learning a technical subject, exploring an idea, or writing code, Prasun AI is designed to provide practical assistance through a simple conversational interface.

Powered by Google Gemini

Prasun AI uses Google Gemini technology to generate responses. Depending on the model available, users may experience different levels of speed, capability, and reasoning performance.

AI technology continues to evolve. As a result, available models, features, and capabilities may change over time as new improvements become available.

What You Can Use Prasun AI For

Prasun AI can be useful for a variety of everyday and learning-related tasks, including:

  • Getting explanations about unfamiliar topics.
  • Learning and exploring new concepts.
  • Brainstorming ideas and organizing thoughts.
  • Writing, rewriting, and improving content.
  • Understanding programming concepts and writing code.
  • Working through technical and general problems.
  • Exploring questions and subjects out of curiosity.

Our Approach

We believe AI should be useful without being unnecessarily complicated. Prasun AI is designed with a clean and straightforward interface so users can focus on what they want to accomplish rather than learning how to use the platform.

The goal is not to replace human judgment or professional expertise, but to provide a practical tool that can help people learn, create, explore, and work more efficiently.

Important Information

Like other AI systems, Prasun AI can sometimes produce inaccurate, incomplete, or outdated information. AI-generated responses should therefore be reviewed carefully, particularly when the information is important or could influence a significant decision.

Prasun AI is not a substitute for qualified professional advice. For medical, legal, financial, engineering, or other specialized matters, users should consider obtaining appropriate professional guidance.

Continuous Improvement

Prasun AI may continue to evolve as we improve its interface, performance, features, and available AI capabilities.

Because the platform relies on evolving AI and third-party technologies, certain features, models, or capabilities may change over time.
