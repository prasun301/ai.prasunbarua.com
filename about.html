About Prasun AI

Simple, accessible and useful AI assistance.

What is Prasun AI?

Prasun AI is an AI-powered assistant created to make artificial intelligence simple and accessible through an easy-to-use web interface.

The platform allows users to ask questions, explore ideas, learn concepts, generate content, solve problems, and receive AI-assisted explanations.

Powered by Google Gemini

Prasun AI uses Google Gemini technology to generate AI responses. Different available Gemini models may provide different balances of speed, capability, and reasoning performance.

Our Goal

Our goal is to provide a clean and straightforward AI experience without unnecessary complexity. Prasun AI is designed to be useful for everyday questions, learning, writing, brainstorming, programming, technical topics, and general exploration.

Important Information

AI technology can make mistakes. Responses generated by Prasun AI should not automatically be treated as authoritative or professional advice.

For important decisions, users should verify information using reliable sources and, where appropriate, consult a qualified professional.

Continuous Improvement

We may continue improving the interface, features, performance, and available AI capabilities of Prasun AI over time.

Features and available models may change as the underlying technology and services evolve.
